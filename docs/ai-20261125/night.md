# Something for the night

Riga is full of good bars with a decent beer selection, and we haven't tried all bars in this list

In Old Town:

-   The Armory Bar - Vecpilsētas iela 11. 

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CRVQSfGoYF7eHw7t8)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://www.thearmourybar.com/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/the-armoury-bar/4540376)
    [:fontawesome-brands-square-facebook:](https://www.facebook.com/TheArmouryBar/)

-   Aussie Backpackers Pub (Vaļņu iela 43) is said to have a nice beer selection.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/97Cg3o4UXgEjsy9MA)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/aussie-backpackers-pub/1070647)

-   The Banshee: Hot dogs and beer. Skarnu iela 11.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kR6MNQPbqag7qK7i7)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://www.thebansheeriga.com/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/the-banshee/11248889)

-   KAĶIS MAISĀ, Torņa iela 4. Bar created by the TEIKA brewery. (The author of this list has been there in 2026.)

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/R8etUTLibBvmjrnw9)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://www.kakismaisa.lv/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/kakis-maisa-local-craft-beer/11288693)

-   Mans Alus - Salons MyBeer. Alksnāja iela 10/12.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vUNhqBQhxHDMEykFA)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/mybeer-mans-alus/10643373)

-   Ms&Mr Beer, Mazā Miesnieku iela 1. Beer, pizza and snacks.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gxy2mjrV7DfeXZRD9)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://www.msmrbeer.lv/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/ms-and-mr-beer-taphouse/9156322)

-   The Thirsty Bulldog sports bar, Vecpilsētas iela 8a.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yht1fz9gqdkHQnTH9)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://www.thethirstybulldogriga.com/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/the-thirsty-bulldog-sports-pub/11178749)

    In the same building there is also [Distillers Republic](https://www.distillersrepublic.com/) if you're more into Gin.

-   Two More Beers: Vagonu iela 21. Restaurant and bar with a nice outside terrace in summer.
    Good food and a good selection of beers (the author of this list has been there a few times.)

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/TVxF6Gsf5twGTAxi9)
    [:fontawesome-solid-link:](https://twomorebeers.lv/en/)
    [:fontawesome-brands-untappd:](https://untappd.com/v/two-more-beers/10083272)

-   For Belgian beer there is Bon-Vivant the Belgian Beer Cafe which is also a restaurant.

    [:material-google-maps:](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3xgVD5GhyGGkua8X6)
