# Local information

## Venue

The hackathon takes place in the main headquarters of CSC, located in the 
Live Science Center Keilaniemi, Keilaranta 14, 02150 Espoo, Finland.

[Click here for directions on how to get there from Helsinki](https://csc.fi/en/about-us/contact-information/#espoo-directions-and-accessibility).


## Hotels

There are two hotels within walking distance of the Life Science Center:

-   [Heymo 1](https://www.sokoshotels.fi/en/hotels/espoo/heymo-1)

-   [Radisson Blu](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/hotels/radisson-blu-espoo)

In the city centre, hotels close to the metro line are the most interesting option.
This includes the hotels close to the central station.



## Some restaurant suggestions for the night

-   On the Aalto University campus close to the CSC building:
    [Ravintola Fat Lizard Otaniemi](https://www.ravintolafatlizard.fi/en/etusivu-english/)
    is a restaurant run by the local Fat Lizard brewery serving mostly pizza and burgers
    (though they have some other dishes also), with - of course - a selection of their beers.

    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/imcFdNCzL1ZxWomXA))

-   [Bryggery Helsinki](https://helsinkibryggeribrewhouse.fi/en/)
    is a brewery restaurant close to Esplanadi.

    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/s2NCiWz9ar9pghMS7))


## Drinks in the evening

-   On the Aalto University campus close to the CSC building:
    [Ravintola Fat Lizard Otaniemi](https://www.ravintolafatlizard.fi/en/etusivu-english/)
    is a restaurant run by the local Fat Lizard brewery serving mostly pizza and burgers
    (though they have some other dishes also), with - of course - a selection of their beers.

    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/imcFdNCzL1ZxWomXA))

-   [Kaisla](https://www.raflaamo.fi/en/restaurant/helsinki/kaisla): More a bar, but some simple
    bar dishes if you're not too hungry. Nice beer selection though. And next to a metro station,
    so easy to reach even if you stay near the CSC premises.

    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HNuQYBrX4qNCDmNe9))

-   [Ravintola Juova Hanahuone](https://www.facebook.com/juovahanahuone): The name suggests
    it is a restaurant, but I haven't seen people eat there a lot. They do have a nice beer selection,
    including 27 taps.

    ([Beer menu](https://untappd.com/v/juova-hanahuone/10578290))
    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JHaB1fwtinD8ZprX6))

-   [Roudaribaari IHME](https://untappd.com/v/roudaribaari-ihme/11239436):
    A bar with a very special interior inspired on music and rock 'n roll.

    ([Beer menu](https://untappd.com/v/roudaribaari-ihme/11239436))
    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zJjqEahJUNySPiHi9))

-   [Pien Shop & Bar](https://pien.fi/en/) is a craft beer shop right in the center of Helsinki
    where you can also drink beers.

    ([Beer Menu](https://untappd.com/v/pien-shop/5214359))
    ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ptKsVp4d6DABXfaLA))
